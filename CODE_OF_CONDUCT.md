# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Critique ideas and code, not people. Everyone is welcome, whatever their background or experience.

Not allowed: harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual content, sharing someone's private information, and repeated disruption. Don't pressure anyone to share private designs, credentials or account details.

These rules apply in the project's issues and other spaces it manages, and to the maintainer too. The maintainer may remove content, lock or close discussions, or block accounts.

Report a problem privately to [support@foliodesign.app](mailto:support@foliodesign.app) with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports stay private.
